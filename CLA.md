## How to accept

Accept this agreement once, by adding this trailer to a commit in your first
pull request:

    MycorX-CLA-Accepted: v1.0

The easiest way is to let git write it:

    git commit --trailer "MycorX-CLA-Accepted: v1.0"

or, on a commit you have already made:

    git commit --amend --trailer "MycorX-CLA-Accepted: v1.0"
    git push --force-with-lease

CI checks for it, and tells you what to do if it is missing. Once your pull
request merges you are recorded in
[CONTRIBUTOR_LEDGER.md](CONTRIBUTOR_LEDGER.md) and never asked again.

It is a commit trailer rather than a comment because a merged commit cannot be
quietly edited afterwards, and this agreement is only worth as much as the
record of it.

If you are contributing in the course of your employment, your employer may own
the copyright in your work. In that case please have someone authorized to bind
your employer contact MycorX at info@mycorx.com.au before you submit, so a
corporate agreement can be put in place.
